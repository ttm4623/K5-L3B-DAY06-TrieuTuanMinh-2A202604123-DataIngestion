DAY 06 - KET QUA DANH GIA RETRIEVAL
===================================
Trang thai: CHUA CO KET QUA BENCHMARK DA XAC MINH

Luu y:
File nay ghi lai cac du kien da kiem tra va cac so do con thieu. Khong dien
Hit@1/Hit@3 hoac diem cham thu cong khi chua co trace va nhan tu mot lan chay
co the tai lap. Khong dung cac gia tri duoi day nhu ket qua retrieval.

1. DU LIEU DA KIEM TRA
---------------------
- Corpus: starter/data/pg39272-images.html (co trong workspace).
- Nguon theo data/manifest.json: Project Gutenberg eBook #39272,
  "The Inventions, Researches and Writings of Nikola Tesla";
  manifest khai bao 43 don vi.
- Ket qua goi load_html_corpus trong moi truong hien tai: 1 don vi,
  page_id rong, 967347 ky tu. So lieu nay khong khop voi 43 don vi trong
  manifest; can sua/kiem tra parser truoc khi tin cac so do retrieval.
- Benchmark data/tesla_benchmark.json: 20 cau, 15 dev va 5 test;
  ca 20 cau co evidence_quote. Khong cau nao co truong filter.
- Da quan sat bang truy van doc-only truoc do: bang chunks co 4570 dong tong.
  Khong co thong ke theo strategy hoac trace retrieval duoc xac nhan.

2. CAU HINH KHAI BAO TRONG MA NGUON
----------------------------------
- A: fixed-size chunk, size=500, overlap=50.
- B: paragraph chunk, max_size=800.
- C: cung paragraph chunk nhu B; code dang loc section="chapters"
  cho moi cau hoi. Day la loc co dinh, khong phai filter theo tung cau hoi.
- Embedding: SentenceTransformer "all-MiniLM-L6-v2".
- Vector store: PostgreSQL + pgvector, cosine distance.
- Database: ket noi local port 55433 theo cau hinh trong starter/src/run.py.

Day la cau hinh trong source, khong phai bang chung rang mot lan benchmark
hoan tat thanh cong.

4. DIEU KIEN DE CO KET QUA TIN CAY
----------------------------------
1. Kiem tra parser de tao dung cac don vi co page_id va lien ket dung
   gold_unit_id trong benchmark; benchmark hien khong co filter field.
2. Chay cung mot model, corpus, store va bo cau hoi cho A/B/C; luu top-3
   trace co chunk_id, text, score, metadata, gold va nhan match.
3. Chi sau khi trace duoc tao moi tinh Hit@1/Evidence Hit@3 tach dev/test.
4. Cham thu cong chunk coherence, metadata va grounding; ghi mau, nhan,
   ly do, nguoi cham va han che.
5. Ghi so chunk, thoi gian ingest/truy van va chi phi (neu co), cung voi
   moi truong va lenh chay. Khong ghi de len du lieu database hien co khi
   chua duoc phep.

Ket luan hien tai: chua the xep hang A/B/C hoac tuyen bo chien luoc tot nhat.
Can khac phuc sai lech parser/manifest va tao trace co the kiem tra truoc khi
bao cao cac metric.
